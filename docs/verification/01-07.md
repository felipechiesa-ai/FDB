## Implemented, not verified on screen

- Adding a player to a team (`MembershipEditorView`). The player picker searches the store as you type, after a 200 ms pause. A newer search cancels the older one, and a cancelled search never replaces the list.
- Editing a membership's shirt number and status from the team roster and from the player's team list (`EditMembershipSheet`). The player, the team, and the dates stay as recorded.
- Ending a membership from the roster context menu (`EndMembershipSheet`).
- The roster list, ordered by shirt number, and the player's team list with links back to the team.

## Still missing

- Editing a membership's dates or notes. The edit sheet changes only the shirt number and status.
- Filtering the player picker by position or archive status. The picker searches by name only.
